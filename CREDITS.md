## Art

No third-party art ships in the game any more.

**AI-generated art (Robert, 2026-10-25):** the character and enemy models, the card art and the battle arenas were created largely with AI image tools, then cut out, arranged and edited by hand. The rest (interface, rarity gems, card backs, effects, animation, some canvas-drawn scenery) is drawn in the game's own code. This is shown on the credits page, and the Steam store disclosure must match it (`docs/12_STEAM_READINESS.md` §8).

Removed (no longer used, assets and cutting scripts deleted): the Jitsu-Koan collection and TheRealFusion's Ghostwire, Tania Herrero's *Monstrous Menagerie #1*, Maaot's *Mossy Cavern*, wigglesmith79's cave pack, Dumivid's *Mechanized Magic* (gems and card back are drawn in CSS now) and SovDigital's *Free Magic Effects* (the fire, frost and portal sprites became coloured pulses).

If a third-party asset is added again, list it here and in `CreditsScreen.tsx`.
