# UX1 review: duel UI batch (inspector, controls, bot, HP/Damage swap)

Scope: apps/web/src play/{Inspector,keywordHelp,bot,bot.test,DuelView,Prompts}, play.css, cards/{Card.tsx,card.css}, duel/scene/Monsters.ts, hand/{Hand.tsx,fanLayout.ts}. Plus session.ts / view.ts for context. Source not modified.

Counts: CRITICAL 0 / HIGH 3 / MEDIUM 8 / LOW 8

## CRITICAL
None. No hidden-information leak found: hoverBoard reads only the redacted `viewRef` and drops any card with `cardId === '?'` (DuelView.tsx:361-365); hoverHand only comes from the human's own hand; the bot reads only `view`/`legal`.

## HIGH

1. **Inspector body is clipped on typical laptop heights, so stats/keywords are unreadable.** play.css `.inspect` (~line 587-599): `max-height: calc(100vh - 13rem); overflow: hidden`, with a fixed 270px-wide Card (378px tall). At 1366x768 (about 650px viewport) the body gets about 50px, so the HP/Damage row and keywords are cut off mid-line; at 800px tall only 1-2 keywords fit. This is the whole point of the reader. Fix: render the card smaller when height is tight (e.g. width = clamp(190, (innerHeight - 13rem) * 0.45, 270)), or give `.inspect__body` its own `overflow:auto` region (grid rows `auto minmax(0,1fr)`), or put the text beside the card instead of under it.

2. **Possible soft-lock: a response window with only Pass legal and no UI to pass.** session.ts `pump()` (~lines 171-178) skips the auto-pass when `sparkEnablesResponse(state)` is true, but that function only checks printed costs of a face-down trap or Swift card against mana+1; it does not check that Spark is still in hand or in `legal`. After Spark is played (or for the first player), any window with legal == [pass] while a trap costs exactly mana+1 gives `winOpts = []`, so DuelView.tsx:383 `showWindow` is false: no WindowPanel, no End turn (no endTurn in legal), the turnbox shows "Your move", and `stuck` is false because legal.length is 1. The player cannot proceed (the bot would pass via `view.window`). Pre-existing, but it is exactly the "unable to end turn" case. Fix: in `pump()` require that the Spark play is actually in `legal` before holding the auto-pass; and/or in DuelView show the WindowPanel (with Pass) whenever `view.window?.priority === HUMAN && legal.some(pass)`, regardless of `winOpts.length`. Add a session test. (Reasoned from code, not reproduced.)

3. **Player-facing "Power"/"Guard" still appears in card rules text, contradicting the HP/Damage swap.** About 27 occurrences in packages/content/src/cards/*.ts, rendered by Card.tsx:240 and the Inspector, e.g. aurelion.ts:68 "restore 2 Guard", :303 "4 or more Power", :344/:388 "+1 Power"; nexus.ts:50/132; umbral.ts:127; wyldmaw.ts:92/149 (and others). The frame says DMG/HP while the text says Power/Guard. Fix: reword card text to Damage/HP (update docs/02_CARDS.md, docs/01 section 3.9 "Power/Guard" templating and 3.8 Corrode/Stun wording, log in DECISIONS.md, fix any text tests), or map at render time in `parseRules` (fragile).

## MEDIUM

1. **Hover state goes stale and can stick (inspector).** DuelView.tsx:351-372 updates only on `pointermove` over `.duel__stage` and returns early when `id === hoverId.current`. (a) No `onPointerLeave` on the stage and nothing clears it when the pointer moves onto HUD/hand/modals (siblings, not children), so the last hovered monster stays shown; the 270px inspector (z35) can then sit over the ActionBar (`left:1.25rem; bottom:8.3rem`, z30) just when the player moves toward it. (b) Because the id is unchanged, a buff, damage, death or Ambush flip under a stationary pointer never refreshes `stats`/`def`; a dead unit's card stays up. Fix: add `onPointerLeave` that clears `hoverId` and `hoverBoard`; store the hovered key and derive the info from `view` with `useMemo`, clearing it when the card is gone.
2. **Hand hover sticks after playing a card.** Hand.tsx:259-266: when a card is dropped/played its slot unmounts and `onPointerLeave` is not guaranteed to fire, so `hoverHand` keeps the played card; DuelView.tsx:520 gives hoverHand priority over hoverBoard, so board hover is blocked until another hand card is entered and left. While dragging, the reader also sits over the left slots. Fix: clear hoverHand in `onPlay`/`onDragStart` and when it is no longer in `handCards` (effect), and hide the inspector while dragging.
3. **Keyboard users cannot read cards.** Hand slots are focusable (Hand.tsx ~237) but the inspector is driven only by pointer enter/leave. Add `onFocus`/`onBlur` to call `onHover`; the Inspector `aside` is not announced. Board units are Pixi-only, so there is no keyboard path to them.
4. **Corner controls overlap the hand at 900-1200px wide.** play.css `.corner` (right 1.25rem, bottom 1.25rem, z45; End turn min-width 12rem plus Settings/How row and hint) sits above the hand (z20). handCardWidth is 140px below about 1270px and maxSpread = 0.62*vw, so the fan reaches about 0.81*vw/2 + 70 from centre: at 900px the corner's left edge (about 688) overlaps the last 1-2 cards (to about 799); at 1200px the last card is overlapped by about 55px. Overlapped cards cannot be hovered or dragged. The `.self` panel (left, z30, 15rem) does the same on the left. Fix: cap `maxSpread` to `vw - 2*(cornerWidth + margin)`, or shrink the corner under 1200px (End turn `min-width:auto`, hide kbd and `turnbox__hint`), or move Settings/How to play to the top bar.
5. **E shortcut gaps** (DuelView.tsx:399-410): no `!e.repeat` (a held E can end the next turn the moment control returns); no `defaultPrevented`/`isComposing` check; ends the turn with no confirmation even when playable cards remain; fires while the Settings panel is open (acceptable). Stale closures are fine: the effect has no deps array, re-subscribes every render and reads current `inputOpen/end/pending/chooser/showHelp/showWindow`; it correctly ignores input/select/textarea, the mulligan/optional/choice prompts (pending), the chooser, Help and the response window, and the game-over/stuck state (no endTurn in legal). Suggest `if (e.repeat || e.defaultPrevented) return;` and a one-line guard that requires a second press when `playable.size > 0`.
6. **Modal accessibility** (Prompts.tsx:15-25, DuelView.tsx:522): `role="dialog" aria-modal` but no focus move/trap, no Escape to close (HelpPrompt, Concede chooser), background stays tabbable. HelpPrompt renders before the mulligan/choice modals at the same z-index (80), so a prompt appearing while Help is open hides it. Fix: autofocus primary button, Escape handler, `inert` background, close Help when a pending prompt appears.
7. **Keyword help inaccuracies** (keywordHelp.ts): Corrode (97-100) "Loses 1 damage" reads as taking damage; rule (docs 3.8) is "loses 1 Power, minimum 0": say "Its Damage drops by 1 at the start of its controller's turn (minimum 0)." Ambush (101-104) omits "or flip later in your Main for cost - 1". Guardian omits "face-up only". The rest (Blitz, Shield, Ward, Pierce, Lifebind, Veiled, Stun, Devour, Evolve, Assemble, Overclock, Resonance, Echo, Ascend, Scry, Swift, Quick, Negate, Charges) match section 3.8, and the `Record<Keyword,...>` is exhaustive against types.ts:15-37.
8. **Inspector placement**: `.inspect` (left 1.25rem, top 4.4rem, 270px) overlays the first unit slots at 900-1100px widths when hovering board cards near the left edge, hiding the card being inspected. Flip it to the opposite side based on the hovered slot, or anchor it beside `.self`.

## LOW

1. DuelView.tsx:88 `search.includes('bot')` matches any query containing "bot" (`?robot`, `?seed=abbot`). Use `new URLSearchParams(location.search).has('bot')`. With `?bot` absent, no interval or timer is created (both effects return early), so nothing leaks in production; with it, the interval adds 33ms per tick on top of gsap's own ticker, so animations run about 2x in a visible tab. The bot is reachable by any player in production; harmless, but consider gating on a dev flag.
2. bot.ts cannot loop forever in tests (finite plays/attacks, `steps < 900` guard), but in the browser if the engine rejects its pick with a `RulesError`, session.ts re-offers the same legal list and the effect (DuelView.tsx:422-429) picks the same action every 250ms indefinitely. Remember the last rejected action and fall back to endTurn/pass. `enemyOnly` (bot.ts:191-196) treats unknown ids (player ids) as own; `chooseOptional` always accepts (may Devour its own units). It reads only the redacted view and own-card costs: no hidden info.
3. bot.test.ts:272 `winner === HUMAN || 1 || 'draw'` is vacuously true once `over`; assert something meaningful. The keyword test (282-288) only checks text length and never compares with the engine's Keyword list.
4. Inspector.tsx:50: `token` cards get no TYPE_HELP (key is only 'unit'); sigils show nothing; relic charges are not shown.
5. Inspector.tsx:55 `KEYWORD_HELP[k]` would throw if content ever carried a keyword outside the type; guard with `?.`.
6. A11y nits: End turn accessible name becomes "End turn E" (mark kbd `aria-hidden`); `.turnbox__wait` has no `aria-live`; Settings button lacks `aria-controls`; focus is lost when End turn is replaced by the wait text; `aria-label` on the plain `.card__stat` div is ignored by many screen readers (use `role="img"`).
7. Mulligan cards are 190px wide (Prompts.tsx:99): the row wraps below about 1100px, and at 720px tall the Confirm button can fall below the fold of the scrolling panel. Consider `width = min(190, (vw - 96) / n)`.
8. HP/Damage swap verified consistent in Card.tsx/card.css (HP left blue with "HP", Damage right orange with "DMG", aria-labels "HP n"/"Damage n"), Monsters.ts:207-209 (guard left, power right, same colours), Inspector, and HelpPrompt. Remaining `power`/`guard` identifiers are code-only. Only the card rules text (HIGH-3) and docs still say Power/Guard. The Inspector mini-card shows printed stats while the text row shows current ones; label the row "now".
