# Knight before the balance pass of 2026-10-29 (frozen; commit 5fe78b0)

Reward-eligible Knight cards: **52** (rewardPool, his own cards; basics and tokens excluded). All cards: 54.

Trait: **Hold and Answer**: Start every fight with 6 Barrier. Guard: when your Barrier stops an enemy attack hit completely, that enemy is left Open until the end of your next turn (one Opening per enemy). The first time each turn you earn an Opening, gain 3 Barrier.

Starter: strike, strike, strike, strike, defend, defend, defend, defend, sundering-blow, briarplate

## Rules as they stood

- Guard: an enemy attack hit of 1 or more that her Barrier stops completely leaves the attacker Open (one Opening per enemy); it goes at the end of her next turn (decay). Only a Guard block fires the 'opening' trigger; leaveOpen cards do not.
- Riposte (counter damage or the riposte op) spends the target's Opening; only a single-target damage op could Riposte (to === 'target'); ordinary attacks and 'if Open' conditions leave it.
- Thorns cards give temporary Thorns (statusTheirs, until the enemy turn is over); nothing removed Thorns early.
- Giant's Stance, Deflecting Blow and Sentinel's Patience read enemy attack intent ('attacking', 'foesAttacking'), which is always 0 against a hero in the Arena.
- Barbed Vigil (2: 11 Barrier, 4 Thorns) overlapped Ember Ward (2: 12 Barrier, 4 Thorns).
