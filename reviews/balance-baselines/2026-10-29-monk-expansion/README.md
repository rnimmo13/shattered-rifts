# Monk before the balance pass of 2026-10-29 (frozen; commit 780256c)

Reward-eligible Monk cards: **51** (rewardPool, the class's own cards; basics and tokens excluded). All cards: 53.

Trait: **Martial Forms**: You fight in a form, starting in Current. Once each turn, completing your form's technique gives 1 Mastery and 3 Barrier.

Starter: strike, strike, strike, defend, defend, defend, still-guard, planted-heel, kindle-fury, turning-palm

## Rules as they stood

- Martial Forms: Root (two different Defensive techniques), Current (a Defensive then a Striking technique of another card), Ember (two different Striking techniques at one enemy); completion at most once a turn: 1 Mastery and 3 Barrier (the trait)
- Mastery capped at 3, kept between turns, at most 2 spent a card; starts in Current
- 6 Auras: Perfected Motion, Grandmaster's Patience, Unbroken Calm, Breath Between Blows, The River Turns, Unmoved Mind
