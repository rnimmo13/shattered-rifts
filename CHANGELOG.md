# Changelog

Player-visible changes, newest first. Format loosely follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com).

## [Unreleased]

### Added (newest)
- **About 300 cards get their own art** (every class; 169 cards still to go).
- **The Necromancer is stronger early.** Grave Chill now Raises 3 at the start of every turn, so a Bone Thrall is standing for your Command cards from turn 1 (and every one that falls still curses an enemy with Fate). Bone Bulwark (Raise 5, 8 Barrier), Bone Sift (Raise 5), Grave Robbery (6 damage), Spirit Walk (needs 6 cards) and Toll in Advance (5 Mana) are better.
- **Fixed:** the Sharpen tooltip names the Old Twelve Gauge on the Drunkard's cards (Scope, Reload, Hair Trigger...), not the Obsidian Blade.
- **Upgraded Auras do more, instead of just costing less.** 62 Auras now upgrade the way Slay the Spire's Powers do: a bigger number (Rampage of Ire+ deals 9, Daily Rite+ gives 3 Fervor, Masquerade+ 4 per suit), Opening hand for small engines (Shell Game+, Battle Rhythm+, Legion+), or a smaller cost in HP (Ancient Fury+, Berserk Tempo+). Auras whose effect can't grow (Unholy Pact, Command Chip, Ascendant Form...) still cost 1 less. The Knight's are unchanged.
- **New looks for the Monk and the Trickster.** The Monk is a bare-handed, shaved-headed monk with fire around his fists (no more scimitars), and the Trickster is a masked stage magician with a fan of glowing cards. Spotter Drone, Generator, Bulwark Walker, Scrap Golem, Dreadnought, Pup and Pack Alpha have their own art.
- **The Drunkard is rebalanced and learns to Stagger.** Stagger cards are a drunk's swing: they hit a random enemy and hit harder for it (Haphazard Hook, Drunken Lurch, Stumble, Free-for-All, Drunken Master, Blind Drunk). Fewer Buckshot Shells (Load Shells gives 2), drinks give more for their Hangover (Barroom Brawl 16 to every enemy, One for the Road draws 3), the self-damage Auras are trimmed, the gun Auras are cheaper, and two new utility cards: Rummage the Bin and Sway.
- **Fixed:** beating a Warden shows one page of rewards, the Warden's Gift with its Hearts. An extra card reward earned in that fight (Marked for Death) is one more card on the Gift instead of a second page.
- **The Barbarian is now the Monk.** Same fighter and cards (her palms, prayers and stances always were a monk's); Red Mist, Cornered Beast, Blood on the Sand and Berserk Tide are Burning Breath, Coiled Serpent, Iron Body and Storm of Fists. Saved runs and decks carry over.
- **The Barbarian is rebalanced** against the card costing guide. Inner Fire now gives 1 Fervor for each stance you enter (no Barrier), Still Guard gives 3 Barrier, the Auras that pay for every stance change cost more or pay less, and about 40 cards move towards their budget (the weak ones up: Rage of Ages, Final Account, Radiant Heart...). All-Seeing costs 2.
- **Fixed:** clicking a panel title folds it again; the Full Trick goes off after the card that earned it; Suits cards are no longer offered to other Riftwalkers.
- **Fixed: Backup Core** brought a companion back every time it died, so it soaked every hit; it now revives one companion a turn.
- **The Trickster plays with Suits.** Every card is a ♠ (Attacks), ♥ (Barrier), ♦ (cards and Mana) or ♣ (Traps and debuffs). Four pips beside the Trickster light as you play each suit in a turn, and the card you're aiming shows which one it would light. Light all four for a **Full Trick**: 5 damage to every enemy and a card. The Patience build path is now Suits, with new cards (Flourish, Card Fan, Card Sharp, Cut the Deck, Queen of Hearts, Ace of Clubs, Wild Card, Royal Flourish, Showstopper, The Joker, The Whole Deck) and a new Masquerade. The starting deck has Pick a Card in place of a Defend. Banked Breath, Coiled Patience, Sandbag, Waiting Game, Measured Breath and Fade Away are retired (saved decks get the closest card).
- **Move your panels:** your Auras, the other side's Auras and the battle log can be dragged by their title, resized from the corner and folded; double-click a title to put it back. Where you left them is remembered.
- **Card rarities have one set of colours everywhere:** uncommon blue and rare gold, as on the cards, on the saved decks page too.
- **The Trickster is rebalanced** against the card costing guide: a starting Set the Web of 7 and 1 Exposed, about 35 number changes towards each card's budget (Cripple, Perfect Alibi and the two big Traps come down; Mirror Veil, Trap Net, False Floor, Poison Pantry and the Trap Auras get cheaper), and hand-written upgrades. Cheap Shot (needs a Trap set for its Exposed) and Stalk (needs an Exposed target for its draw) are new versions, no longer copies of Assassin cards. Deep Pockets costs 2. Death Trap and Whisper Net are retired (saved decks get Bait and Switch and False Floor). A Trap that cancels now says "Cancel that hit": it stops the one hit that set it off, as Shielded does.
- **Sweeping attacks are marked:** an enemy move that strikes every unit on your side (Cleave, Dirge, Orbital Strike...) carries a red "Hits all" tag, and every unit it will hit gets a red marker and a glowing HP bar. A move whose hits land at random says so too.
- **The grimoire opens over the whole window** wherever you look at cards (your deck, a pile in a fight, choosing a card to upgrade or remove, an event that asks for a card). Cards always stay within a readable size range and fit on the page; they no longer grow, shrink or jump when you resize the window, open a ribbon or turn a page. The arrow keys turn its pages.
- **Event options that show cards** put their words on the left and the cards on the right, with a tag saying what happens (Removes, Adds, Swaps or Upgrades a card) and a strip on each card (Removed, Added, Out, In, Upgraded).
- **The Arena button** on the map is a glowing button before your Deck: green when your deck is in the Arena, red and struck through until your first fight saves it.
- **The aiming arrow locks onto its target:** over anything you can choose (an enemy, or your own companion when scrapping one to make room), its point snaps to the target's centre, a ring closes round it and the target lights up.
- **The Engineer is rebalanced** against the card costing guide, and her machines come in three **frames**: a Light drone takes 1 of your 3 companion slots, a Heavy walker 2 and a Colossal war machine all 3 (pips on the card and the machine). With no room, a summon scraps companions to make room, and **Salvage** hands their Might, Primed and half their HP to the new machine. New: the Engineer's own guns (Pulse Pistol, Arc Welder, Laser Grid, Particle Cannon), Targeting Uplink, Full Salvo, Deploy Spotter, Bulwark Walker, Scrap Golem and Dreadnought. Nanoswarm, Drone Uprising, Overload and Mob Rule are retired. A card that summons two companions now needs room for both. New utility: Battery Pack (1 Mana), Schematics (pick a card from your draw pile) and Build Generator, a machine that powers your next turn.
- **Enemies are a little gentler at every Rift Tier:** Tier I starts at 90% of their HP and damage, and each tier and stage adds less.
- **The Hunter heals 2 HP after every fight she wins** again. **Attunement** is an Aura.
- **Overflow is tanked by the rest of the pack:** what is left of a hit that kills a companion goes to your next companion first, and reaches you only when none is left. **Blood Pact** is an Aura.
- **The Assassin's Sleeve Knives are Daggers** (and Inked Knives are Inked Daggers). **Fixed:** a Trap whose damage kills the attacker no longer puts its Exposed or Faltering on another enemy.
- **The Hunter is rebalanced** card by card against the new card costing guide, and goes hunting: four new Traps (Bear Trap, Snare Line, Deadfall, Baited Snare), Hunting Grounds, Single Out and Run It Down. Her summons are smaller and cheaper to read (Pup 5 HP, Pack Alpha 12, Dire Wolf 18), Litter brings two Pups, and Crimson Pact and Unbridled are retired.
- **Might only boosts attacks:** damage from Auras (Blood Fire, Serpent's Coil, Bastion Engine...), totems and Traps is the number on the card; Might, Exposed and Faltering only change attack damage, as their text says. Thorns and "when attacked" Traps only answer attacks. **Fixed:** Blood Fire showed twice in the Aura list.
- **Three cards made whole:** Fated Hand lets you choose the 2 cards from your draw pile (they come next turn), Army of the Drowned raises two Thralls, and Marked for Death gives an extra card reward when it kills. **Fixed:** Bloodfeast's max HP (and Marked for Death's reward) is paid even when that kill ends the fight.
- **No starting card pick:** a run starts with your Riftwalker's usual deck and goes straight to The Threshold.
- **Riftwalkers go by their class for now** (Knight, Engineer, Hunter, Trickster, Assassin, Shaman, Barbarian, Spellblade, Necromancer, Drunkard), and the **Trapper is now the Trickster**.
- **Fixed:** a Free Attack, Free Skill or free Aura is no longer used up on a card that cost nothing anyway; Blade Echo and replayed cards also repeat cards that ask you to choose.
- Sylas is rebuilt on the Slay the Spire 2 Silent's card list under his own names: Sleeve Knives, Bleed and sleight of hand, with Sly cards that play themselves when you discard them. He starts with 5 Strikes, 5 Defends, Hobbling Jab and Duck and Weave at 70 HP; his old cards in saved runs and decks become the nearest new card.
- Obsidia is rebuilt on the Slay the Spire 2 Regent's card list in her own theme: Arcana for Stars, the Obsidian Blade for the Sovereign Blade, Retainers and Neutral cards for minions and Colorless cards; 82 cards, a 75 HP Regent starter, and her old cards with no counterpart retired into the nearest new one.
- **Dorian is now the Necrobinder's card list, card for card** (85 cards of his own plus Strike and Defend): Raise brings Bone Thralls with the HP the card says (up to three, then the weakest grows), Command cards make every Thrall strike, Gravesouls draw cards, and Fleeting cards have payoffs; 70 HP, and a starting deck of 4 Strike, 4 Defend, Raise Thrall and Off the Leash. His other cards and his Trap are gone.
- Ilvane is now the Slay the Spire 2 Defect, card for card, under his own names: totems work like the Defect's orbs (Hawk, Bear, Elder, Owl and Splinter), Spirit is Focus, 75 HP and a starter of 4 Strikes, 4 Defends, Hawk Call and Twin Awakening; his Elements and Rites cards are gone (old saves keep a close replacement), and status cards (Haze, Scorch, Muck, Drain) are new.
- **The Totemist is now the Shaman** (Ilvane).
- **Valeria is now the Ironclad's card list, card for card** (82 cards of her own plus Strike and Defend): her Thorns, Traps and other own cards are gone, Hot Temper, Pact of Embers and Inner Furnace are new, and several numbers match theirs (Ember Ward costs 2 for 12 Barrier, Immovable Oath gives 30 Barrier, Bastion Engine deals 5 to a random enemy, Dawn Smite is 5 damage and 5 Barrier, Oath of Iron no longer Holds).
- **A run starts with two uncommon cards you choose** from your Riftwalker's own (they replace two plain cards). The three build-path starting decks are gone.
- **Build paths are hidden for now:** cards show no build path, and offered cards no longer say whether they fit your build. Character screens list the mechanics each character uses instead.
- **Valeria has the rest of the Ironclad's cards** (29 more: Wild Gamble, Unravel, Bitter Breath, Fan the Flames, Howl of the Fallen, Raised Hell, Juggler's Rhythm, Bloodfeast, Crippling Blow...), and her older cards were trimmed to a pool of 95. Grim Focus draws 3 and then no more cards this turn; Break Guard hits twice against an Exposed enemy.
- **New card rules:** play a card from the top of your draw pile (or your consumed pile) for free; "no more Mana / cards this turn"; "if ..., then ..." cards; Sapped (an enemy hits for less through its next turn); Cruelty, Sturdy, Unmovable and Free Attack; max HP gained from a kill is kept for the run.
- **Valeria is rebuilt as an Ironclad-style fighter. Devotion is gone,** and so are the healing and judgment cards that fed it (they turn into a card that does the same job in old saves and Arena decks). She starts with 5 Strikes, 4 Defends and **Sundering Blow** (8 damage, 2 Exposed), and has five build paths: Bulwark, Thorns, and the new **Might** (Twin Cut, Challenge, Whirling Blade, Mounting Fury, Warlord's Oath...), **Consume** (Burn It All, Rite of Recall, Unholy Pact...) and **Blood** (Draw Blood, Scar Over, Blood Fire, Bloodied Plate...). New: Mastered Strike counts every Strike in your deck.
- **No Legendary cards for now:** the old Legendaries are Rares, rewards, Warden Gifts and the Spin to win top prize give Rares instead, and the Arena has no Legendary limit to meet. Legendary Boons are unchanged.
- **Characters can have more than three build paths** (Valeria has five).
- **Rivet:** only Assemble, Relay Strike and Command Chip still make companions act now (once each); the rest of Assemble charges them up with Primed, so their next strike hits harder. Companions now spend Primed.
- **Ilvane:** every damage number on his cards is 1 higher; the Hawk totem deals 4 a turn (was 3) and Awakens for 10 (9), Splinter Awakens for 7 (6).
- **Rivet:** cheaper companions have less HP: Patrol Drone 5 (was 7), Micro Drone 3 (4), Sentry Turret 7 (10), Repair Bot 4 (6), Enforcer Mk.II 12 (14).
- **Rivet:** Swarm and Core pulled apart. Swarm goes wide (Heavy Plating, Repair Bot and Reactor join it); Core goes tall: new Crankshaft, Reinforced Frame and Heavy Hitter grow with your biggest companion, and Reactor Meltdown deals the HP of the ally it blows up. Salvage Bot, Swarm Protocol and Scrap Volley are retired.
- **Valeria:** Devotion costs show just the number; Sunfall and Sunforged Edge show an X (they spend it all). Sunfall strikes 2 different random enemies.
- **Companions grow:** a heal on a companion gives it that much max HP instead (Rivet's Tinkerer, Sync Pulse, Primal Feast...).
- **New starting kits:** Valeria (Bulwark: Bulwark Sweep, Shield Charge, Tower Shield; Thorns: Mirrored Aegis, Thorn Volley, Barbed Oath; Devotion: Sacred Vow, Hymn of Reckoning, Lightbearer) and Rivet's Swarm (Overcharge Blast, Call Turret, Breach Charge).
- **Resolve:** losing a fight with Resolve left retries it at full HP (was at least half).
- **KP:** the Hangover build path is rebuilt around drinking: drinks (Liquid Lunch, Swig, One for the Road, Bottoms Up...) pay off now and leave a **Hangover**, HP you lose at the start of your next turn (never your last). Bottle to the Head, Bender and Haymaker hit harder the more you carry, Hair of the Dog and Sweat It Out clear it, and his heals (Sob Story, Liquid Dinner, Pass Out, Bottomless Flask) grow with the HP you are missing. Glass Trap, Glazier, Spare Change, Double Glazing, Trolley Barricade and Glass Underfoot are retired.
- **Ilvane:** an upgraded Gathering Storm grows with the rest (it made a new copy instead).
- **Ilvane:** the Whittling build path is now **Elements**: lightning, ice, fire, water and earth spells that grow with Spirit, Gathering Storm that grows every time you cast it, and the Legendary Avatar of the Elements.
- **The last Warden leaves a Gift too:** beating the stage 3 Warden offers rare cards of your class or a Warden's Heart, like the others. A Warden's Gift always has cards.
- **Ilvane:** the Bear totem gives 2 Barrier at the end of your turn (was 1).
- **Leaderboard:** a campaign run could be left off the board after a busy day of play (refused reports used up the daily limits); now only recorded runs count against them.
- **Devotion** costs show a ⚜ (it used a cross that read like a plus: "✚ 4" looked like "+4").
- **Valeria:** Unbroken Wall costs 3 (2 upgraded). Retribution costs 2 (1 upgraded).
- **Shielded** units glow inside a yellow bubble until the shield is spent.
- **Fortified** no longer stacks; cards say "Gain Fortified".
- **Valeria:** Sunfall (10 +2 per Devotion) and Sunforged Edge (6 +1 per Devotion) spend all your Devotion. The Devotion orb's label fits.
- **Spin to win:** the three Rift-crossed cards are laid out side by side.
- **Campaign header:** fits smaller screens (two lines on a laptop, three on a phone); the Arena deck is a small ⚔ badge beside Deck.
- **Cards:** the build path label sits at the bottom left.
- **Bigger, rarer pools:** every Riftwalker has 20 commons, 30 uncommons, 25 rares and 3 Legendaries, split evenly over the three build paths, so a rare is a build-defining find. 16 new cards, and many earlier cards back.
- **Kesh:** the Feast build path is now **Predator**: gain Mana, draw hard and play many cards in one turn. 16 new cards, and Apex Frenzy as its Legendary.
- **Cards show their build path** in a label on the card.
- **Card pools trimmed:** every build path has at least 8 commons, 7 uncommons, 5 rares and a Legendary, plus a few cards for any build, near-duplicates removed (63-71 cards each, down from about 90). A removed card in an old run or deck becomes a similar card of the same build path.
- **Starting kits:** each kit is now two uncommons and a common of its build path.
- **5 copies at most:** a deck holds at most 5 copies of a card, in a run and in the Arena (a Legendary is still one of a kind).
- **Legendaries reworked:** every Legendary belongs to a build path and builds around it. Dawn's Mercy (was Bastion of Dawn), Eye of the Storm, Shatterstorm, Grave Harvest, The Spirit Council, Bloodmoon Hunt, Masquerade, The Twelve-Gauge Opera, Death by Inches and others now do much more than a one-off burst.
- **New cards:** KP's Gun (Buttstroke, Spare Barrel, Gun Oil, Dead Eye) and Ilvane's Whittling (Antler, Serpent and Moth Carvings, Whittler's Kit, Endless Whittling).
- **Kesh:** no longer heals after a won fight.
- **Valeria:** her Devotion shows beside her Mana. Her Devotion starting deck now has Hymn of Reckoning (6 Devotion: 10 to every enemy) instead of Ward of Ages. New Devotion attacks: Hand of Justice (4 Devotion: deal 10, Exposed), Consecrated Blade (8 Devotion: deal 9 twice, 6 Barrier) and Hand of God (15 Devotion: deal 36).
- **Fight result:** Continue sits on top, the replay options under it.
- **Card library:** filter a Riftwalker's cards by build path.
- **Kesh rebalanced:** Pack Instinct heals 2 after a won fight (was 6) and she no longer starts each fight with 1 Might. Her Pack starting deck has Savage Command instead of Rally the Pack, and her Feast starting deck Gnaw instead of Devour (both can still be found as rewards).
- **Valeria: Devotion is a resource.** Whenever she heals she gains that much Devotion, even at full HP. New cards spend it: Lightbrand (5 Devotion: deal 14), Hymn of Reckoning (6 Devotion: 10 to every enemy) and Sunfall (10 Devotion: 2 damage for every Devotion you had, then it is all spent). Sunforged Edge now grows with your Devotion, and Last Stand counts as a Bulwark card.
- **Sanctified cards are final:** the shrine's chisel and the engraver no longer put a tag on a sanctified card.
- **Kesh:** the 1 Mana Pup card is called Summon Pup and its upgrade stays at or below the Hound. Den Mother deals its damage to the enemy that killed your ally.
- **Kesh:** Alpha Call brings a 16 HP / 10 damage Alpha. Den Mother is now an Aura that deals 15 damage to a random enemy whenever one of your allies dies (20 upgraded). New: Whelp (1 Mana, a Pup) and Dire Wolf (3 Mana, a 24 HP / 14 damage Dire Wolf).
- **Kesh:** Rend loses 2 HP, Blood Frenzy loses 1 HP, and Savage Bite deals 8 (10 upgraded).
- **Kesh:** the Hound has 8 HP and hits for 4 again, she can have 3 companions again, Litter is back (two Hounds), and Rally the Pack gives each of your allies 4 Barrier.
- **Overflow:** when an attack kills one of your companions, all of the damage left over carries through to you, so cheap minions no longer block a whole big hit.
- **Kesh:** Devour deals 16 (was 14); Fell the Prey, its near twin, is switched off for now.
- **Your history follows you:** signed in, your run history, your Arena match history and your username appear on every device, as your runs already do.
- **Litter** is switched off for now: it is no longer offered (a deck that has it keeps it).
- **Kesh:** Call Gnashlings is gone (old decks get Thick Hide), and Litter summons 2 Gnashlings.
- **Kesh** can have 2 companions at once (was 3).
- **Kesh:** the Hound has 5 HP and hits for 3, Savage Bite deals 6 (8 upgraded), Feeding Time gives 3 max HP.
- **Stun cards** apply only Stun (no extra Exposed); rare and Legendary ones Stun for 2 turns. Card text says how many turns.
- **Kesh:** every card of hers that costs HP costs 1 more (she has Lifesteal now). Heel, Howl and Guard Dog are gone: a saved run or deck that holds one gets Call Gnashlings, Pack Tactics or Rend in its place.
- **Rally the Pack, Hamstring Slam and Second Death** no longer make your allies act: each of your allies gains 3 Barrier instead.
- **Stun reworked:** a Stunned unit no longer skips its turn. It counts as Exposed, Faltering and Brittle at once (they never stack with the real ones) for 1 turn, or as many as the card says.
- **Arena: choose your deck after you are matched.** Every saved deck comes to the match, and before each game you pick one, of any Riftwalker, without seeing your opponent's (they cannot see yours either). One deck is enough. Players are matched by Arena rank.
- **Kesh:** her Hound has 8 HP. Her starting deck has Rend (Deal 9, lose 2 HP, gain 1 Might) instead of Guard Dog, and Savage Bite deals 8 (10 upgraded).
- **Stunned heroes:** in the Arena and against a ghost Warden, a Stunned hero draws one card fewer at the start of their next turn (before, it did nothing to a hero). Companions and enemies still skip their next action.
- **Campaign board:** the run state is the last column, called Status.
- **Traps are called Traps everywhere:** Master Trapper, Gleeful Trapsmith and Curtain Call said "Snare". "Trap" is now bold on cards, with its note on hover.
- **Deep Pockets** and every card that raises your maximum Mana: the extra Mana starts on your next turn. Deep Pockets+ costs 3 (not 2) and has Hold and Opening hand.
- **See upgrades on hover:** the card library, your Grimoire and every deck view show the upgraded card beside the one you hover.
- **Sandbox:** the book is no longer split by build path, and ticking Upgraded (+) keeps you on the same page.
- **Battle log:** an enemy move shows its damage ("Gilded Squire uses Slash (9)"), and every hit or heal shows the HP before and after ("(HP 40 → 34)").
- **Clearer Auras:** an Aura that gives you a status once now says it lasts the rest of the fight (Unchained: "Gain 3 Might for the rest of the fight"), and cards that give you a status say "Gain", not "Apply".
- **Kesh toned down:** cards that call her pack now bring a companion by their rarity: a Pup (6 HP) from Guard Dog and Heel, a Hound (10 HP) from Rally the Pack, a Pack Alpha (14 HP) from Alpha Call. Her companions share half her Might (rounded down) instead of all of it.
- **Rift Resonance** gives 1 Might, and 1 more every time your deck is reshuffled (it gave 2 Might once). Auras can now answer a reshuffle.
- **Arena:** see how many players are looking for a match before you queue. Making or joining a friend's room has its own panel, and the room code box shows its hint in full.
- **Constructed decks removed:** the Arena only ever takes decks saved from a campaign run.
- **One Legendary Boon per run:** once you hold a Legendary Boon, no other one is offered, sold or given for the rest of the run.
- **Kesh reworked:** her companions share her Might, she heals 6 after every won fight, and her basics no longer only cost HP (Savage Bite has Lifesteal, Blood Frenzy hits every enemy, Guard Dog brings a Hound). Her self-damage cards pay off properly now, and look-alike commons were pulled apart.
- **Lifesteal:** some attacks heal you a share of the HP they take off.
- **Companions that grow:** some of Kesh's, Rivet's and Dorian's cards raise a companion's max HP instead of only shielding it.
- **Wardens are gentler on Resolve:** losing to a Warden costs 1 Resolve (an Elite still costs 2), and every Warden fight shows how much Resolve you have and what a loss would leave.
- **Ammo Dump** adds 6 Buckshot Shells (was 8); upgraded, 6 upgraded shells. **Fire in the Hole+** scales faster (+2 per 3 consumed cards) instead of gaining flat damage.
- **See the random cards before you choose:** an event option that trades, transforms, copies, gives, removes or upgrades a random card now shows the exact cards: what leaves your deck, and what you get.
- **Hit numbers you can read:** damage (red), healing (green) and Barrier (blue) pop up on whoever takes them, bigger, and hold still while they fade instead of flying off; a hit that Barrier partly soaks shows both numbers.
- **Cards show what they will really deal:** a damage number on a card in your hand counts your Might, stance, Faltering, Sharpen and Edge, green when higher than printed and red when lower; aim it at an enemy and its Exposed counts too; a card that could hit several enemies shows the range. A sharpened weapon shows its sharpened damage right on the card.
- **Bigger status numbers** on the buffs and debuffs under each fighter, and the map legend is just the names.
- **Cheaper upgrades for the big cards:** The Twelve-Gauge Opera+, Rift Collapse+, Bloodmoon Hunt+, The Great Pole+ and The Perfect Trap+ cost 2 Mana; Shatterstorm+, Cataclysm+ and Killing Glass+ need one less Arcana; Cataclysm Totem+ awakens your totems 3 times.
- **Card rewards retuned:** each card after a fight is 55% common, 35% uncommon, 9% rare and 1% Legendary; after an Elite 35 / 42 / 20 / 3. How to play shows the one table (card luck is gone).
- **Your Arena deck is at stake:** a run saves its deck for the Arena as you go. Fall, or give up in the middle of a fight, and that deck leaves the Arena; give up anywhere else and it stays. Push deeper for a stronger deck, or stop and keep it.
- **"Save", not "bank":** decks are saved for the Arena, everywhere it is mentioned.
- **Sandbox:** the Riftwalker picker is the same one as on the home page (figure, build paths, trait and the row of icons), and the Grimoire uses the width of the window, so it shows a full two-page spread.
- **Shorter Arena games:** each game of a match is called at 15 minutes (was 20) for whoever has the larger share of their HP left.
- **Never forced to take a card:** when an event lays out three cards (the smuggler's crate, the cards from other worlds), you can take none of them and keep your deck as it is. Event options are also easier to read (bigger text).
- **Allies show their next move:** every ally and minion wears a small badge with what it does at the end of its side's turn and how much damage it will deal to the front enemy (Might, Weak and Exposed counted), or "Stunned" when it will skip.
- **The map keeps its secrets:** a fight or an Elite only says Duel or Elite until you arrive, and a ghost Warden in the campaign never says whose deck it is.
- **Rift Tiers climb evenly:** Tier I enemies start at 125% HP and 100% damage, and every Tier up adds the same +50% HP and +20% damage (+12% HP and +5% damage a stage) all the way to Tier XX (How to play shows the table and the steps).
- **Legendary Boons** glow crimson instead of gold, so they never look like a common one.
- **Smarter ghosts:** a ghost deck now thinks through the end of its turn (its allies, Traps and end-of-turn effects) before it picks a line.
- **Much tougher enemies:** every enemy has 50% more HP at every Rift Tier.
- **Weapons:** heroes now strike with their own weapons. KP swings his hammer or fires the Old Twelve Gauge (with a muzzle flash), Valeria cuts with her sword or bashes with her shield, Sylas throws his knife, and everyone else has theirs, with painted dust, sparks and spirit swirls where the blow lands.
- **The card you play** shows beside the discard pile instead of over the arena, and the Draw, Discard and Consumed piles wear card backs.
- **Arena games always end:** from Turn 51 both heroes lose more HP every turn (10, then 20, 30...), straight through Barrier and Shielded, and each game is called at 20 minutes for whoever has the larger share of their HP left. A game clock shows under the turn clock.
- **No more blank cards:** a card picture that fails to load falls back to its painted art, and a painting that comes out empty (a browser short of memory) is painted again instead of staying blank.
- **Thorns only answer attacks:** your Traps, Auras, totems and potions no longer hurt you when they hit an enemy with Thorns. A companion that attacks one still takes the Thorns itself.
- **How to play lists every Omen and Warden's Heart:** what each Omen does when it arrives and by Tier XX, and what each Heart gives and costs. The Tier XII Omen is now called Blood Rites.
- **Harder from the start:** Tier I enemies have about 15% more HP and hit a little harder, and every Rift Tier after it climbs faster.
- **The fight title says the Rift Tier** as well as the stage and floor.
- **See what you are up against:** in a campaign fight the Rift Tier's Omens sit at the top right with what each does on hover, and in the Arena (and against a ghost Warden) your opponent's Auras and Boons show there too.

### Changed (the big one)
- **Combat is rebuilt like Slay the Spire.** Your Riftwalker is a fighter on the field with HP; you play cards with Mana (3 a turn,
  a 5-card hand); every enemy shows what it will do next as an icon and a number; Barrier soaks damage until your next turn; and
  fights are side to side, your hero and companions on the left, the enemies on the right. With one possible target a card just
  plays: no aiming. Companions fight for you (Rivet's drones, Kesh's Hound) or buff you; Maren and Vesper go without. Traps became
  Snares: face-down sigils that spring on their own when the enemy attacks, summons, or hurts you.
- **Runs carry HP between fights.** Win a fight and you keep the HP you ended with; shrines heal, upgrade a card (Temper) or remove
  one. Losing a fight costs Resolve and you retry at the HP you entered with. Maps, rewards, the merchant, Wardens and Bank/Continue
  are the same shape as before.
- 4 Riftwalkers, about 80 cards (each with a "+" version), 12 companions and 26 encounters including all four Wardens and Vorthax.
