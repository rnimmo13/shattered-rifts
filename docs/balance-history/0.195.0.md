# Balance assessment 0.195.0

2026-11-04 · rules 79 · Local implementation, unreleased · source: Working tree based on 92466e2; decision 595

Theorycraft assessment of plausible developed decks at comparable upgrade quality. No balance simulations or real-match dataset used. Grades include the class pool and base trait; the marginal Ascendancy is discussed separately. A deterministic fixture establishes behavior, never a win-rate or a tier grade.

Measured matches used: 0. Target: Three or more distinct competitive A-band play styles per class in both modes; campaign XVII–XX extremely difficult but beatable. This is an acceptance target, not established parity.

Grades are estimates of complete deck strength, not isolated passive value. A+/A/A− are competitive sub-bands; B indicates a meaningful reliability problem; S flags a suspected outlier. They are not win rates. Ascendancies unlock after Tier III. Developing = IV–X, deep = XI–XVI, endgame = XVII–XX. Fight opening = turns 1–3; extended = turns 6–10. These bands describe different things.

## Reasoning and next checks

### Paladin

Retention gives useful defense immediately and supports scaled Barrier attacks later. Strong late potential depends on converting defense into damage. Confidence: low–moderate.

Watch: Piercing damage, direct HP loss and slow kills against scaling foes.

Evidence: Authored rule/card review and hand costing; docs/14_CARD_COSTING.md; packages/content/src/v2/ascendancies.ts; packages/content/src/v2/sharedBalance.test.ts (implementation only).

### Marshal

The extra Reserve and percentage payoff grow with upgraded attacks, rather than adding a fixed early-only bonus. Confidence: low–moderate.

Watch: Time spent banking; the opening hand may not access Reserves efficiently.

Evidence: Authored rule/card review and hand costing; docs/14_CARD_COSTING.md; packages/content/src/v2/ascendancies.ts; packages/content/src/v2/sharedBalance.test.ts (implementation only).

### Blood Knight

A meaningful payment now charges even at large Arena HP. The real-HP-loss rate remains and the bank boosts all hits of the next Attack. Confidence: low–moderate.

Watch: Multi-hit burst stacking; campaign attrition and healing access.

Evidence: Authored rule/card review and hand costing; docs/14_CARD_COSTING.md; packages/content/src/v2/ascendancies.ts; packages/content/src/v2/sharedBalance.test.ts (implementation only).

### Swarmwright

Earlier Fabrication improves ordinary swarm growth. Consume recycling prevents the former repeatable free loop from defining its ceiling. Confidence: low–moderate.

Watch: Area damage, summoning Mana costs, command density; unrestricted copy/reclaim engines still need observation.

Evidence: Authored rule/card review and hand costing; docs/14_CARD_COSTING.md; packages/content/src/v2/ascendancies.ts; packages/content/src/v2/sharedBalance.test.ts (implementation only).

### Golemancer

Recovery reduces the loss of a grown machine without removing the existing HP-to-damage conversion. It still needs time and a replacement card. Confidence: low.

Watch: Focused companion removal and setup time remain its largest uncertainties; recovery does not refund summon Mana.

Evidence: Authored rule/card review and hand costing; docs/14_CARD_COSTING.md; packages/content/src/v2/ascendancies.ts; packages/content/src/v2/sharedBalance.test.ts (implementation only).

### Artillerist

## Corrections and limits

- All 30 estimates fall in the A band overall; Golemancer and Deathwish remain low-confidence A−, not proven repaired matchups.
- Opening grades can remain B+ for a slow build while its complete-deck estimate is A−; no claim that every opening hand is equally strong.
- No measured playtests used. Do not manufacture win rates or turn fixture success into A-tier evidence.
- Storm Sovereign still does not trigger from passive effects; damage is uncapped, only Barrier is limited per copy.
- Card-pool parity remains unresolved. Efficient draw/Mana, rare access, seat order, player skill, deck size and campaign investment affect results.
- Reroll uses original odds and can show repeated cards. It improves choice access, not guaranteed synergy or rarity.
- Build examples are descriptive, not predefined reward groups or an exhaustive list of viable decks.
