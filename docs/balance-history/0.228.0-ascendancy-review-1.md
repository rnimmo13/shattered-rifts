# Balance assessment 0.228.0

2026-11-08 · rules 96 · Local implementation authorized; no release approval · source: Robert's direction (2026-11-08) after Claude's hand-priced late-game/Arena review of all 30 Ascendancies flagged Totemist and Artillerist as runaway scalers and Trapmaster, Reaver, Bladedancer, Blademaster, Cardsharp and Deathwish as flat or weak. No player decks, fights or exports; no simulations.

Decision 685. Totemist: no opening 1 Spirit / 8 Barrier; Cultivation gives at most 5 Spirit a fight. Artillerist: +2 Might on its first 6 turns only (12 total), 6 Barrier on first Attack unchanged. Trapmaster: 2 more Trap slots (5), draw 1 + 2 Barrier per Trap kept, Traps +5% damage per own Trap sprung before it this fight, up to +50%. Reaver: also +1 Might on the first own HP payment each turn (a more distinctive design is still wanted). Bladedancer: Daggers +10% per Dagger played before it this turn, up to +50%, resets each turn. Blademaster: first Sword each turn also gives +1 Keen after resolving, from 1 up to 5. Cardsharp: Full Trick also gives 1 Might. Deathwish: bonus keeps growing below 40% HP to +40% at 10% (was flat +25%). Hand-priced with docs/14_CARD_COSTING.md; grades below are review signals, not measured results. Rules 96 restarts unfinished battle checkpoints. Decision 686: Sage also draws 1 on its first Serene exit each turn, and Enlightenment gives 1 Might on its first 5 such exits and 1 Bracing on the first 3. Sunwalker Dawn: 2 Might on each of its first 5 Radiant entries (10 total); Radiant frequency and the Fervor return cap of 4 unchanged.

Measured matches used: 0. Target: Arena first: distinct competitive styles at1000HP; typical10+ own turns. Drago optimized-deck target roughly50% or somewhat above, pending representative human/deck evidence.

Campaign I-III starter survival, IV-VII setup, VIII-X consistency, XI-XII finale; fight turns1-3,6-10 and11+. Arena1000HP equal seats; improved Shaman starting pressure without measured parity. Chip reduction from faster kills is an inference, not a measured result.
