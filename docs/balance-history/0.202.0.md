# Balance assessment 0.202.0

2026-11-05 · rules 83 · Local implementation; not pushed or deployed. Campaign acquisition changes only; Arena unchanged. · source: Base fc12f2b4343ccc535e25140aa42256f0aa920ce5 plus local 0.202.0 changes in packages/campaign/src/{eventsMore,eventsTuned,events,run,deals,save,types}.ts; exact ledger in CHANGELOG.md and player patchNotes.ts.

Static acquisition review, not new combat ratings. Treasure is now guaranteed by next-tier entry (inside the requested three-tier deadline); legacy maps and Tier XX deliver at the next stop. Rare choice +105 shards and alternate +8 max HP/25% max-HP heal retain their actual values. Broker and Grimoire random rewards become three-card choices; Soldiers adds an 8-HP common choice and Ash Market a 60-shard uncommon choice. More consistent cross-class selection benefits freely mixed engines and rare-dependent setups, but no specific synergy is guaranteed. Campaign I-III starter access improves; IV-X, XI-XVI and XVII-XX all gain the same event options. Existing saved event options remain frozen. Opening/extended strength, setup and recovery remain conditional on the actual cards selected. Arena and growth beyond own turn ten are unchanged and unmeasured. Representative mixed builds: companion HP plus foreign healing, Exposed plus foreign multi-hit, Consume plus foreign draw. All remain unrated in both modes and every progression band. Zero simulations or human matches were run for this revision.

Measured matches used: 0. Target: Arena first: distinct competitive styles at1000HP; typical10+ own turns. Vorthax optimized-deck target roughly50% or somewhat above, pending representative human/deck evidence.

No fights were evaluated in this revision. Deterministic fixture checks establish delivery, rewards and save compatibility only. Combat labels are carried forward without new grades or parity claims. Developing = IV-X, deep = XI-XVI, endgame = XVII-XX. Opening, extended and beyond-ten-turn strength remain unmeasured.
