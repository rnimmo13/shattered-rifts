# Balance assessment 0.213.0

2026-11-06 · rules 89 · Local candidate; implementation authorized, release approval required · source: Two retained historical client-observed Campaign positions, inspected and copied separately under .wrangler/testing-0.213-2026-11-06/real-campaign. Neither contains Breaching Strike. No relevant real-player Arena exports or current Knight Campaign observations available; no simulations run.

Requested promotion of one new common Strike: Breaching Strike Common -> Rare, Mana 1 -> 2, damage 7/10 -> 18/24, Exposed 1 -> 2. Both modes. Hand budget: 18 + 2×3 = 24 versus a 2-Mana Rare target of 19×1.25 = 23.75 points; upgrade 24 + 6 = 30. This is a heuristic, not measured parity. Raised Hell still auto-plays for no Mana. The 33 new arena paintings change presentation only.

Measured matches used: 0. Target: Arena first: distinct competitive styles at1000HP; typical10+ own turns. Drago optimized-deck target roughly50% or somewhat above, pending representative human/deck evidence.

Grades are estimates of complete deck strength, not isolated passive value. A+/A/A− are competitive sub-bands; B indicates a meaningful reliability problem; S flags a suspected outlier. They are not win rates. This revision assigns no new grades. Ascendancies unlock after Tier III. Current Campaign assessment bands are I–III, IV–VII, VIII–X and XI–XII. Fight opening is turns 1–3; extended play includes turns 6–10 and growth beyond ten own turns, addressed in the assessment notes.

- Historical Campaign saves are client-observed positions, not independent matches or complete reward histories. They contain no tested card and support no promotion grade.
- No class letter grades are changed. All thirty Ascendancies, class starts, free mixing and long-fight recovery remain unmeasured for this revision.
