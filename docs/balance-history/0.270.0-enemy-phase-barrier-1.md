# Balance assessment 0.270.0

2026-11-13 · rules 112 · Local implementation · source: Robert’s report about enemy Barrier at half HP, Campaign Omen tuning, and deterministic combat scenarios.

Campaign enemy phases grant the same Barrier and Might at the same HP thresholds. Previously the triggering attack stopped at the threshold, leaving the full Barrier. Now damage past the threshold spends the Barrier immediately and any excess reduces HP. A strong hit can reach multiple thresholds or finish the fight. Example with 100 max HP, 60 HP, 20 phase Barrier and a 40-damage attack: previously 50 HP and 20 Barrier remained; now 40 HP and 0 Barrier remain.

Measured matches used: 0. Target: Typical Arena games of 10 or more own turns and roughly 50% or better Drago clears with optimized decks remain playtest targets.

No new Campaign grades. The reported 50% phase behavior identifies a rule issue. Available local report previews are fixtures and supply no current human observations. The carried Arena grades are hand estimates from the #768–769 baseline.
