# Balance assessment 0.215.0

2026-11-06 · rules 89 · Release approved by Robert, decision 662; validation pending · source: Two retained historical client-observed Campaign histories (Assassin and Golemancer) inspected and copied under .wrangler/testing-0.215-2026-11-06/real-campaign. They predate this topology revision and do not measure route-offer selection under it. No relevant real-player Arena exports or new-topology Campaign positions available. No new matches or simulations.

Decision 661, explicitly included in release approval 662: fixed convergence every six floors becomes an independent 8% meeting chance on each middle floor. Otherwise lane width drifts -1/0/+1 within 2-5, or chooses 2-5 after a meeting. Five starts, two final shrines, stops per path and node-kind/reward definitions remain. The sequence/count of available alternative stops changes, so practical opportunities to find a merchant, optional fight or event can change even with unchanged per-node rules. Saved maps remain intact. Art/checkmarks are presentation only. Preserve all prior unmeasured combat grades; deterministic topology checks establish graph properties, not strength or player behavior.

Measured matches used: 0. Target: Arena first: distinct competitive styles at1000HP; typical10+ own turns. Drago optimized-deck target roughly50% or somewhat above, pending representative human/deck evidence.

Route-acquisition review only: Campaign I-III class starts, IV-VII setup, VIII-X consistency and XI-XII finale preparation. Arena opening, turns 6-10 and 11+ own turns retain prior unmeasured status. No new combat grades, human outcomes or simulations are reported.
