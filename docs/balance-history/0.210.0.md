## Corrections and limits

- Earlier simple common-budget claims are superseded: Elites now receive an explicit 25% HP / 8% damage premium after tier scaling, rounded to integer percentages.
- HP-derived enemy healing and Barrier also rise under the existing conversion; this is not HP-only durability.
- A higher Tier I baseline adds ten HP and three damage percentage points at EVERY tier; it is not a multiplicative 10% global increase.
- Available client Campaign save positions are old-route observations, not independent measured matches, current-rules tests, or complete reward histories.
