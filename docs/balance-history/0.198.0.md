# Balance assessment 0.198.0

2026-11-04 · rules 81 · Implemented release assessment; Robert authorizes push/deploy after checks. Projections, not measured parity. · source: Based on released 50bc92a (0.197.0/rules 80); decision 603, release 0.198.0/rules 81.

Static rule/card review and hand arithmetic after all approved long-fight proposals, including Bonelord and Deathwish buffs. No simulations or measured match dataset. Unchanged rows carry forward estimates with relative-strength uncertainty.

Measured matches used: 0. Target: Distinct A-band styles in both modes, typical competitive Arena games ten-plus turns per player; proposed working band 10-14, not a hard minimum or a measured result. Campaign XVII-XX difficult but beatable.

Grades are estimates of complete deck strength, not isolated passive value. A+/A/A− are competitive sub-bands; B indicates a meaningful reliability problem; S flags a suspected outlier. They are not win rates. Ascendancies unlock after Tier III. Developing = IV–X, deep = XI–XVI, endgame = XVII–XX. Fight opening = turns 1–3; extended = turns 6–10. These bands describe different things.

## Reasoning and next checks

### Paladin

Retention supports defense and Barrier attacks, but constant Barrier generation converges rather than growing forever. More turns help only if the deck increases its generation or converts it into a finish. Shared Crimson Offering delays one Mana; Scent now caps each Aura at three hostile Exposed draws per owner-round. Overall grade carried forward; relative matchups may improve as the suspected outliers slow. Confidence: low; static estimate, zero measured matches.

## Corrections and limits

- This snapshot follows 0.197.0/rules 80; all older JSON/Markdown/CSV assessments remain unchanged. All approved long-fight changes are now implemented, including candidate Bonelord and Deathwish protection. P4 remains as previously released.
- All 30 overall central estimates are A-band hypotheses. Shatterer and Deathwish explicitly retain B+ downside risk. Removing S labels is a projection from slower growth, not evidence of measured parity; A+ ceilings and setup-dependent weak openings remain.

- Opening/extended/long grades describe conditional fight development, distinct from campaign tiers. Class starts precede Ascendancies. Representative mixed builds do not restore predefined build groups.
- No hero HP, enemy damage, minion targeting or clock changes in this release. No simulations or measured matches; fixture checks establish timing/values only. Compare real matches by version, seat, matchup and deck quality, and preserve future corrections as new snapshots.
