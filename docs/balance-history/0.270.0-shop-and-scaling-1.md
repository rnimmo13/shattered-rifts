# Balance assessment 0.270.0

2026-11-13 · rules 113 · Local implementation · source: User reports and live export fetched 2026-11-13: 667 Campaign fights, 6964 Campaign positions, 11 Arena games. Raw files in .wrangler/testing-balance/2026-11-13/.

Merchant card stock rerolls 50 +50 each time per shop. Hunter: Trail of Blood and Bloodsong cost 0->1 both upgrades; Bloodsong immediate Might 2/3->1/2; Pack Frenzy Might 3/4->2/3. Reaping Attack-card Command companion HP conversion 1/2->1/4 rounded up; hero half unchanged. Shatter held Spell Power halves above 20; Obsidian Aegis scaling halves above +10 Barrier. No HP changes.

Measured matches used: 0. Target: Typical Arena games of 10 or more own turns and roughly 50% or better Drago clears with optimized decks remain playtest targets.

Historical observed evidence, not new measured parity: 65 Spellblade fights all rules108, before rules109 diminishing returns. WJKHm6jFwn (Drago Tier12) recorded a 2158-damage turn8 attributed to lo~starforged-blade+; nGS3r3KxdN recorded 1823 on turn5 including 1376 Blade and 447 meteor-jab+. Anonymous player hashes cannot confirm sacacweeda attribution. These observations establish historical high ceilings, not a current exploit. No replay resimulation or balance simulations run.
