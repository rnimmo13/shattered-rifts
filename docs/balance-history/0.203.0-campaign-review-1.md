Supersedes the earlier local fixed-order/no-late-Omen assumptions in 0.203.0.json, which remains archived. New runs save a shuffled permutation of all eleven Wardens for I–XI without repeats. XII independently shuffles those eleven, then Vorthax. Bosses formerly encountered late may now be first; each style’s early survival against every possible Warden needs real evidence. Normal fight-to-Elite promotion changes round((tier−1)*100/18)% to min(100,(tier−1)*10)%: II6→10%, V22→40%, X50→90%, XI56→100%. Existing naturally placed Elites are additional; event ambushes use the same promotion. The last pre-finale tier has no plain fights but retains noncombat stops. Per-Elite reward fade retains its prior endpoint19, detached from promotion threshold, so per-tier rewards are unchanged. Omens now introduce Rallied Foes I (wasIV), Second Wind II, Times Are Tough III, Rift Scars IV (wasV), Dread V (wasVI), Rift Static VI (wasVII), Ironclad VII (wasVIII), Brittle Wards VIII, Blood Rites IX, Third Phase X (wasXIII), Vigilant XI (wasXII), Hunted XII (wasXV), The Rift Awakens XII (wasXX), plus new Fraying Ward XII. They stack and use existing growth formulas from the new unlock tier. Rallied Foes at XII is10.5% Barrier versus9%; Third Phase is now active with11% Barrier/1Might at quarter HP in XII; boss-own-phase exclusions remain. Fraying Ward reduces each automatic post-victory heal by50%, rounded up: Warden30→15% max HP; Hunter2→1HP; applicable Boon and Elite recovery halved. Permanent max-HP gains/current-HP grants, in-fight healing, potions and full-HP Resolve retries are unchanged. Every final Warden still grants normal +5maxHP, Resolve recovery and Gift/shard rewards. Legacy active tiers retain the old Omen profile and topology until advancing. Resolve already changes the fight seed; real initial-combat fixtures verify a changed shuffle and stable saved retry, not a win-rate improvement. The campaign board adds unspent shards in the ranked run after progress, then arrival time; spending lowers the count. Hoarding may change purchase decisions without changing rewards or prices. Keep the earlier route-count/final-scaling assessment context:410→188 baseline map stops; Vorthax850/185→530/145% HP/damage. All Campaign ratings remain unrated, Arena rules/cards/HP/AI unchanged. Zero new simulations or human matches. Broader difficulty tuning is explicitly a follow-up.

Measured matches used: 0. Target: Arena first: distinct competitive styles at1000HP; typical10+ own turns. Vorthax optimized-deck target roughly50% or somewhat above, pending representative human/deck evidence.

All current Campaign bands are unrated after the route redesign. Class starts I–III; developing IV–VII; deep VIII–X; endgame XI–XII, separating Tier XI acquisition from the Tier XII boss gauntlet. Fight opening turns 1–3, extended turns 6–10 and growth beyond ten own turns require fresh evidence. Arena rules and cards are unchanged: equal 1,000 HP, second-seat opening card only. Historical bot results are conditional and are not new measurements for this version.
