## DECISIONS paragraph

**Sylas is the Slay the Spire 2 Silent's card list, as a baseline (Robert, 2026-10-28: "close copies of the originals, but obviously change names and keep our theme, i will adjust things going forward, but we need good baselines right now") →** like Valeria (#364-#367), an on-purpose exception to the rule against copying another game's card effects, until Robert changes them. Names are all original and the theme stays his: Shivs are his **Sleeve Knives** (tagged `knife`; the Silent's Accuracy is Edge), Poison is **Bleed** (`seared`), Weak is Faltering, Vulnerable is Exposed, Dexterity is Bracing, Strength lost is Sapped, Artifact is Steadfast, and Sly is **Sly** (a new keyword: a card an effect discards from your hand is played for free; the end-of-turn discard does not count). **Source:** the Silent card list on slaythespire.wiki.gg as edited 2026-10-27, which includes the v0.110.0 beta branch (July 2026) changes: Haze costs 2 and applies Weak, Sidestep replaced Scare, Expertise draws 2 that Retain, Echoing Slash is Uncommon. Costs the wiki did not state were taken from metabot.gg card pages. (The knight was copied from v0.103.2; the two lists are about three patches apart.) **Bleed = Poison, checked:** at the start of the bleeding unit's turn it takes its stacks as unblockable damage, then loses 1, which is what Poison does. One difference: Bleed applied by an enemy follows the difficulty's damage percent (`scaledByDifficulty`), so it is a little gentler on Easy. **Starter:** 5 Strike, 5 Defend (Neutral), Hobbling Jab (Neutralize: 3 damage, 1 Faltering) and Duck and Weave (Survivor: 8 Barrier, discard 1), 12 cards (v2.test allows 12 for him). **70 HP**, the Silent's. **Trait:** Hidden Blades (a Sleeve Knife every turn) is kept, though it is not the Silent's Ring of the Snake (draw 2 more on the first turn): it is an extra Shiv every turn on top of the Silent's list, so it is the first thing to look at if he is too strong. **The cards (Silent card = ours):** basics Neutralize = Hobbling Jab, Survivor = Duck and Weave; token Shiv = Sleeve Knife (Shiv+ = Sleeve Knife+, 6 damage), Inky Shiv = Inked Knife.
