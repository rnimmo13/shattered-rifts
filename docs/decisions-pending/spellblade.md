`discover` can take Neutral cards only and upgraded ones; `fetch` can look `anywhere`; `forge` can scale; `cheapen` can aim at the card itself; `transform` can turn some cards (chosen, or at random, from the hand or the draw pile; a new `transform` choice); `playPile` can `repeat`; card fields `starX` (spends all your Arcana as X), `after` (back to the hand, or on top of the draw pile), `onDraw`, `returnEvery` and `playFromTop`. A random `exhaustCards` may now sit in an Aura (it asks nothing). The pick dialog has the two new choices, the Arcana badge shows X, and the card info explains the new statuses; Sapped's info no longer says it always wears off (Weight of the Crown's lasts). **Deviations, by the engine or the guard rails:** the choices the Regent offers on Photon Cut and Glimmer are made (Glint, Ash Wind), but BEGONE! and GUARDS!!! choose from the hand while CHARGE!!, Cosmic Indifference, Decisions, Decisions, Heirloom Hammer and Tyranny pick at random, Quasar adds one random Neutral card (not a choice of 3) and Foregone Conclusion draws 2 more next turn (so Fated Hand reads like Scheme, Plot); Supermassive is 2 per card created, not 3 (balance.test caps a payoff of something that only grows at 2), and its upgrade adds 3 to the base instead; Heavenly Drill's "+" is X+1 like every X card, not 10 damage; Kingly Kick's cheaper cost lasts until it is played, not the whole fight; Void Form makes the first 2 cards free of Mana, not of Arcana; the Weak and Vulnerable of the Regent are Faltering and Exposed, his Strength loss "this turn" is Sapped until the end of the enemy's next turn, his Vigor is Primed, his Plating is our Plating (as Valeria's Stone Armor); Orbit, Spectrum Shift, Monarch's Gaze, Sword Sage and Seven Stars have no upgrade in the source, so theirs is a Mana cheaper; Glitterstream's upgraded next-turn Barrier (7) is a guess. Two guard rails were widened, with comments: balance.test no longer counts a card with an Arcana cost as free (Alignment, Quasar: each play spends Arcana) nor junk added to the hand as a draw (Collision Course's Debris); the "upgrade changes nothing" test counts Opening hand, Fleeting and on-draw effects (Big Bang, Arsenal, Tyranny, Void Form). Numbers are untested: no sim was run. `RULES_VERSION` should go up (cards and engine changed).

Known limits of the new pieces (from review, left for now): Blade Echo works only for a Blade played from the hand (not one played for free); Barrier put by for next turn (Glass Veil) skips Brittle, Bracing and the "enemy gains Barrier" Trap; on-draw effects fire only on a real draw (not a Foresee pick or a fetch); Free Play counts every card played, Traps too; the damage preview on a card does not count Bladebane or Blade Sweep; the blade statuses' text names the Obsidian Blade although they work on any card tagged `blade`. A "choose any number" transform with no one to ask (a free play) turns nothing.

Noticed on the way, not changed: Valeria's Ember Ward (Flame Barrier) gives Thorns with `temp: true`, which wears off at the end of her own turn, before the enemies attack, so its 4 back never happens.
