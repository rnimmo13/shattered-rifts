Source list: the Slay the Spire 2 Necrobinder, **StS2 v0.111.0** (spire-codex.com data: 88 cards, names, costs, rarities, text and upgrades). Dorian has **85 cards of his own plus Strike and Defend** (Neutral) and two tokens (Gravesoul = her Soul, Baleful Glance = her Sweeping Gaze): her 88 less Strike and Defend (his are Neutral) and **Forbidden Grimoire** (an Ancient Power that removes a card from your deck after the fight; the fight engine has no piece for that). Kept although her game marks them multiplayer-only: Legion of Bone and Glimpse Beyond ("ALL players..." is simply you alone). Protector (an Ancient card) is a Rare. Every card's comment in `pools/necromancer.ts` names the card it copies. **Starter:** 4 Strike, 4 Defend, Raise Thrall (Bodyguard), Off the Leash (Unleash); **70 HP** (hers is 66, below the 70-90 every Riftwalker keeps); he starts with a 5-HP Bone Thrall (her Osty; her starting relic, which Summons 1 every turn, is not copied: his trait stays **Grave Chill**, which does not clash). Mechanics shown: Thralls, Fate, Souls, Fleeting.

**How Osty became several minions:** her Summon N is **Raise N** (`raise` op): a new Bone Thrall rises with N HP while a companion slot is free (3 at most); with all three taken, the weakest companion gains N max HP instead (never a release prompt). Thralls never act on their own (`bone-thrall` has an empty act; an ally with nothing to do no longer acts or shows an intent). Every "Osty deals N" card is a **Command** card (`command` op, tag `command`): **each** living companion strikes, as the actor, so its own Might/Primed/Marrow count and "Osty's HP" is each Thrall's own HP; with no companion nothing happens. "Osty heals N" grows every companion; "Osty dies" sacrifices each (Bone Rain) or the chosen one (Fen Offering). Multiplying Osty's damage by the number of Thralls is the one big deviation to watch in balance.
