**New engine pieces (generic, all with rules tests in `packages/combat/src/totems.test.ts`):** rune fields `start` (planted charge), `decay` (charge lost per passive), `plainEvoke` (Spirit does not add to the awakening), `atStart` (passive at the start of your turn); selector `weakestFoe`; metrics `runeKinds`, `runesOf` and `channeledOf` (with a `rune` on the scale or `when`), `manaSpent`, `attacking`; trigger `evoke` (narrowed by `rune`; its target is each enemy the awakening struck); ops `pulse` (totems' passives now: all, of a kind, or the oldest, optionally at the target), `recost` (a lasting cost change, stored as `CardInst.costUp`, that a play does not spend), `reshuffle`, `returnPlayed`; op options `evoke.last`, `channel` rune `'*'` (random), `draw.keepCost`, `copySelf.free`, `fetch.maxCost`, `exhaustCards.tag`/`everywhere`, `transform.tag` (kind optional), and on Auras `once` (first time each turn), `maxCost`, `times` (it goes after N answers) and `rune`; `created`/`draw` Auras can be narrowed by kind or tag; statuses `echoing` (Echo Form), `boosted` (Signal Boost), `freepower` (Synthesis) and `deftness` (One for All); card fields `inHandAtEnd` (Burn), `onDraw` (Void) and `cheaperOnCreate` (Rocket Punch); a negative status `temp` now comes back at the end of the turn (only what was really taken: Hyperbeam); `runeGone` events carry the row index. One behaviour change: a charging totem's charge used to reset after each awakening; it now resets only when the totem stays (`keep`), so awakening an Elder twice deals its charge twice, as in their game. Also `balance.test` treats a card that costs more each play (`recost` > 0) as unable to loop (Modded), and `v2.test`'s "upgrade changes nothing" check now sees Opening hand and Fleeting.

**Deviations from the Defect's numbers or text, and why:** TURBO (Inner Flame) and Overclock (Feed the Fire) are consumed (balance.test forbids a free card that makes Mana or draws and stays, as for the knight's Bloodletting); Burn (Scorch) costs 2 HP rather than 2 damage that Block can stop (the engine's self-damage is unblockable); Hologram (Spirit Memory) returns a random card, not a chosen one; White Noise (Spirit Whisper) makes its Aura free the next time it is played, not only this turn; Bulk Up's Dexterity is Bracing (+1 to every Barrier gained, totems' too); Buffer is Shielded (it stops the next attack's damage, not any HP loss); Echo Form with 2 stacks doubles the first 2 cards; One for All and Energy Surge help only you (no other players); Chaos and Trash to Treasure pick among all five totems; Thunder's extra hit goes to each enemy the Hawk's awakening struck. **Known limits, left for later:** a status card an enemy puts into your deck (Wound, Static) also sets off Smokestack, Trash to Treasure and Rocket Punch (the `created` trigger has always fired for cards added by either side); Echo does not stack with Reverb or Mirrored on the same card (one replay at most). Also fixed on the way: turn-long status changes are now undone newest first, so a Spirit gain and a Spirit loss in the same turn cannot leave a lasting gain. Numbers are the Defect's and untested: no sim was run. `RULES_VERSION` needs a bump (cards and engine changed).
