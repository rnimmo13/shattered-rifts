# Arena art prompts (generated: the layers the fight screen uses)

The floor stays live (lit and animated by the engine); the art below gives it a backdrop, scenery and a surface. For each realm make **one backdrop** and **one prop sheet**, and make **one floor-tile sheet** for all eight. Attach the Valeria style anchor to every prompt. Save results in `assets-src/character-models` named `<realm>-backdrop`, `<realm>-props` and `floors`.

Realms: aurelion, nexus, wyldmaw, umbral, hollowcrown, gloomfen, midden, rift.

## Floors (one image for all eight realms)

```
Create ONE TILE SHEET image of 8 seamless floor textures for a card-battler game, 2048 x 1024, in a clean 4 x 2 grid of square tiles with NO characters.
The attached image (Valeria) is the STYLE ANCHOR: match its cartoon game-art rendering (bold clean outlines, flat saturated colour, simple cel shading, crisp polished shapes). Do NOT copy her character. Backgrounds are SOFTER and less contrasty than characters so the characters pop in front.

LAYOUT (critical): eight square tiles that touch edge to edge with NO gaps, NO borders and NO labels. Each tile is a flat TOP-DOWN view of the floor material, evenly lit with NO shadows, NO strong highlights and NO objects on it, so that it can be tiled seamlessly (the pattern continues across all four edges). Gentle variation, thin dark joint lines, bold simplified cartoon shapes, medium contrast, slightly desaturated.

THE EIGHT, left to right, top row first:
1. AURELION: white marble flagstones with thin gold inlay lines and a few cracks. Palette: ivory, gold, royal blue, warm orange.
2. NEXUS-9: steel grating and riveted metal plates with painted orange hazard line edges. Palette: steel grey, teal, cyan, hazard orange.
3. WYLDMAW: mossy packed earth with scattered small bones and roots. Palette: crimson, bone white, moss green.
4. UMBRAL VEIL: cracked black-and-white marble ballroom tiles with faint glowing teal cracks. Palette: violet, near-black, spectral teal.
5. THE HOLLOW CROWN: polished black obsidian with glowing ember-orange cracks and violet sheen. Palette: black, ember orange, violet, a touch of cyan.
6. GLOOMFEN: dark mud and old wooden planks with patches of moss and shallow water. Palette: swamp green, ash grey, bone, black water.
7. THE MIDDEN: wet cracked cobblestones with scattered bottle glass and puddles. Palette: rust orange, bottle green, amber, deep blue-black.
8. THE RIFT: dark cracked stone slabs with glowing violet-and-cyan seams. Palette: dark purple, violet, cyan, white light.

NEGATIVE: text, letters, numbers, labels, UI, frame, border, watermark, logo, perspective, shadows, objects, characters, photorealism, gritty textures.
```
