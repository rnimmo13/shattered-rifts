Visual review: subjects appear in the requested order, with clear separation and broadly consistent lighting and outlines. Technical note: the generated backgrounds are near magenta, not exact flat #FF00FF. The cutter must use colour tolerance or normalize the background during extraction. Soft wisps on Free Fleeting and the faded Blade Echo also need care when cutting. Pile sizes are illustrated approximations, not exact card counts; their top-card art has small generated differences. Mirrored cards include decorative backs rather than completely plain backs.
