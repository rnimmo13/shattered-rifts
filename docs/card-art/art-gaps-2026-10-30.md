# Card art gaps (2026-10-30)

Checked against the live card list (948 base cards; upgrades share their base card's art), `apps/web/public/art/manifest.json`,
`assets-src/cards/individual/` and the paused checkpoint in `docs/card-art/checkpoint-2026-10-30/`.
**890 cards had art, 58 had none (they now use borrowed placeholders), and 34 more are flagged for a corrected illustration.**

How the art is wired: a PNG named after the card id in `assets-src/cards/individual/` overrides the older sheet export;
`tools/pack-cards.ps1` exports it to `apps/web/public/art/cards/<id>.jpg` and rebuilds the manifest. Nothing in `individual/`
is waiting to be packed (every live card's PNG is already exported).

> **Update:** these cards now show borrowed art of retired cards as a stopgap: see `placeholder-art-2026-10-30.md`. They still need their own illustrations.

## 3. The extra files in `assets-src/cards/individual/`

- **69 files named `exec-<uuid>.png`** (generation leftovers with no brief):
  - 59 are byte-for-byte copies of files that already carry a card id: safe to delete.
  - 4 are copies of the Thunderbird and Moon Owl totems and spirit forms in `assets-src/weapons/individual-totems/`: safe to delete.
  - 6 are unique and belong to no card yet: four Knight (`exec-06900b74` rock and floating cards, `exec-362a068a` resting with a potion,
    `exec-40cfc942` sword slam, `exec-f7e79926` spinning slash against orcs) and two Engineer (`exec-4873b802` drones and a wrench,
    `exec-f4fd92ee` repairing a drone). Their neighbours in the generation order are Hot Temper, Hammer Fall, Cleaving Throws, Oath of Iron
    and the Shaman starters, so they look like alternates of cards that already have art. Use the Knight ones for new Knight cards (above) or delete.
- **7 PNGs of retired cards**: Ember Bolt, Firebrand, Flare, Immolate, Kindle, Searing Lash, Talon Strike (a Shaman casting fire, with the Hawk).
  They match the Shaman model and could be reused for fire-themed cards that have none: Kindling Ward and Burnt Offering are the closest.
