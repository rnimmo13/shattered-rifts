## Sheet `hunter-01` (legendary first)

```
Create ONE ILLUSTRATION SHEET of 9 separate card-art pictures for a card-battler game (like Slay the Spire), 2400 x 1800, no text.

1. "Apex Maw" (attack): Deal 10 (+4 per Might) damage. Heal 10. Consume. Show a dynamic strike caught mid-action. Make it epic: glowing, richer detail, a grander composition.
2. "Bloodmoon Hunt" (attack): Deal 20 damage to ALL enemies. Heal 15. Consume. Show a dynamic strike caught mid-action. Make it epic: glowing, richer detail, a grander composition.
3. "The Hunger Unleashed" (power): Lose 6 HP. Apply 4 Might to all friends. Apply 2 Cadence. Show a grand symbolic still-life: one iconic object or pose that stands for the idea. Make it epic: glowing, richer detail, a grander composition.

6. "Berserker" (power): At the start of your turn: Lose 2 HP. Gain 1 Mana. Show a grand symbolic still-life: one iconic object or pose that stands for the idea. A little more dramatic and detailed.
7. "Blood Pact" (skill): Lose 6 HP. Gain 1 maximum Mana for the rest of the fight (and 1 now). Consume. Show a defensive or tactical moment, calm and clear. A little more dramatic and detailed.
8. "Bloodsong" (power): Lose 2 HP. Apply 1 Cadence. Show a grand symbolic still-life: one iconic object or pose that stands for the idea. A little more dramatic and detailed.
9. "Carrion Engine" (power): Apply 1 Unbound. Show a grand symbolic still-life: one iconic object or pose that stands for the idea. A little more dramatic and detailed.
```
