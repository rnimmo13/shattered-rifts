# Card art needed: Fault Lines (2026-11-03)

**One picture**, for the Spellblade's new uncommon Aura **Fault Lines** (`fault-lines`, DECISIONS #564): "Whenever you play a Sword or a Spell: Apply 2 Fracture to its target (or the enemy with the most Fracture)." It is the Aura that ties her Fracture to the rest of her deck: every swing and every spell leaves a crack behind.

## How to use this

1. Attach both style images:
   - `apps/web/public/art/cards/collapsing-star.jpg` (a scene with no figure)
   - `apps/web/public/art/cards/hammer-and-anvil.jpg` (the Spellblade and her sword)
2. Paste the prompt below. Check there is no text and the picture fills the frame.
3. Save it as `assets-src/cards/individual/fault-lines.png` (4:3 landscape) and tell me it is in: I cut it into `apps/web/public/art/cards/` and the manifest.

## The prompt

```
Create ONE card-art picture for a card-battler game, 1600 x 1200 (landscape 4:3), no text.
The attached images are the STYLE ANCHOR: match their painted fantasy game-art rendering exactly (crisp dark outlines, rich saturated colour, cel shading with glowing light effects). The background is softer than the main subject so the subject pops.

WORLD: the Spellblade, a warrior of black volcanic glass: white hair, black armour with gold trim, a long black glass sword; her face turned to the viewer or in profile. A volcanic glass desert at dusk: black obsidian, ember-orange cracks, violet sheen, floating shards, purple-orange sky. Enemies are rock golems of cracked dark stone.
THE VISUAL LANGUAGE: SPELL POWER is violet and ember light stored inside the black glass of the sword. FRACTURE is thin glowing ember-and-violet cracks spreading through a target; it is a curse, so show the cracks spreading, not an explosion.

THE PICTURE: "Fault Lines" (an Aura: every sword swing and every spell she makes leaves cracks in her enemy). Use the name only to understand the idea; never write it.
Draw: The Spellblade mid-motion, her black glass sword trailing a violet-and-ember arc in one hand and a small violet sigil glowing in her open other hand. From both the sword's arc and the sigil, thin glowing crack-lines run through the air like veins of light and reach a large rock golem on the right, where they sink in and spread across its chest as a web of ember-and-violet cracks. The lines connect her to the cracks: the feeling of a lasting curse that every move feeds. A calm, deliberate pose, not an explosion. One clear subject that reads at thumbnail size, light from the upper left.

NEGATIVE: text, letters, numbers, captions, labels, UI, card frames, borders, watermark, logo, photorealism, gritty texture, nudity, gore, characters facing away, explosions, the golem shattering.
```
