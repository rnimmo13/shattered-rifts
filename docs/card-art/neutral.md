# Card art: Neutral

40 cards in 5 sheets. Anchor: attach the Valeria image to every prompt.

## Sheet `neutral-01` (ancestral first)

```
Create ONE ILLUSTRATION SHEET of 9 separate card-art pictures for a card-battler game (like Slay the Spire), 2400 x 1800, no text.
The attached image (Valeria) is the STYLE ANCHOR: match its cartoon game-art rendering exactly (bold clean dark outlines, flat saturated colours, simple cel shading, crisp polished shapes). Do NOT copy her character; paint scenes and objects. Backgrounds are softer and less contrasty than the main subject so the subject pops.

LAYOUT (critical): a clean 3 x 3 grid of landscape 4:3 pictures (each about 760 x 570), separated by thin flat dark navy (#14181f) gutters, with NO frames, NO borders, NO captions, NO text, NO numbers. Every picture fills its own cell completely; nothing crosses a gutter. Each picture has ONE clear subject in the middle that reads at thumbnail size, a simple scene behind it, light from the upper left.

WORLD: Cards any Riftwalker can take. World: the shattered rift between worlds: violet and cyan energy, floating broken stone.

THE PICTURES, in this exact order, left to right, top row first. Use the card name and its effect only to decide what to draw; never write the name:
1. "Ancestral Blade" (attack): Deal 22 damage. Consume. Show a dynamic strike caught mid-action. Make it epic: glowing, richer detail, a grander composition.
2. "Ancestral Memory" (skill): Draw 2 cards. Upgrade every card in your hand for this fight. Consume. Show a defensive or tactical moment, calm and clear. Make it epic: glowing, richer detail, a grander composition.
3. "Ancient Pact" (power): At the start of your turn: Gain 1 Mana. Lose 2 HP. Show a grand symbolic still-life: one iconic object or pose that stands for the idea. Make it epic: glowing, richer detail, a grander composition.
4. "Echo of Ages" (skill): Apply 3 Might. Apply 2 Thorns. Consume. Show a defensive or tactical moment, calm and clear. Make it epic: glowing, richer detail, a grander composition.
5. "Elder's Bulwark" (skill): Gain 22 Barrier. Consume. Hold. Show a defensive or tactical moment, calm and clear. Make it epic: glowing, richer detail, a grander composition.
6. "Forebear's Gift" (skill): Gain 2 Mana. Draw 2 cards. Consume. Show a defensive or tactical moment, calm and clear. Make it epic: glowing, richer detail, a grander composition.
7. "Heart of the Rift" (power): At the start of your turn: Gain 1 Mana. Draw 1 card. Show a grand symbolic still-life: one iconic object or pose that stands for the idea. Make it epic: glowing, richer detail, a grander composition.
8. "Lineage" (power): Whenever you play an Aura: Draw 1 card. Show a grand symbolic still-life: one iconic object or pose that stands for the idea. Make it epic: glowing, richer detail, a grander composition.
9. "Rift Collapse" (attack): Deal 40 damage to ALL enemies. Lose 15 HP. Consume. Show a dynamic strike caught mid-action. Make it epic: glowing, richer detail, a grander composition.

NEGATIVE: text, letters, numbers, captions, labels, UI, card frames, borders, watermark, logo, photorealism, gritty texture, nudity, characters facing away.
```
