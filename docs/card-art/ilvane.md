## Sheet `ilvane-01` (legendary first)

```
Create ONE ILLUSTRATION SHEET of 9 separate card-art pictures for a card-battler game (like Slay the Spire), 2400 x 1800, no text.
The attached image (Valeria) is the STYLE ANCHOR: match its cartoon game-art rendering exactly (bold clean dark outlines, flat saturated colours, simple cel shading, crisp polished shapes). Do NOT copy her character; paint scenes and objects. Backgrounds are softer and less contrasty than the main subject so the subject pops.

LAYOUT (critical): a clean 3 x 3 grid of landscape 4:3 pictures (each about 760 x 570), separated by thin flat dark navy (#14181f) gutters, with NO frames, NO borders, NO captions, NO text, NO numbers. Every picture fills its own cell completely; nothing crosses a gutter. Each picture has ONE clear subject in the middle that reads at thumbnail size, a simple scene behind it, light from the upper left.

WORLD: Ilvane, a spirit shaman who calls glowing totems. A stone golem who plants carved totems and calls the spirits inside them to do his bidding. Starts every fight with three totems (Hawk, Bear and Splinter). Totems pulse every turn, then burst when awakened. World: a teal-and-orange foundry of machines: hazard stripes, steel, glowing cyan, sparks and steam.

THE PICTURES, in this exact order, left to right, top row first. Use the card name and its effect only to decide what to draw; never write the name:
1. "Cataclysm Totem" (attack): Deal 10 damage to ALL enemies. Awaken your first 10 totems 2 times. Consume. Show a dynamic strike caught mid-action. Make it epic: glowing, richer detail, a grander composition.
2. "The Great Pole" (power): Gain 3 totem slots. Apply 3 Spirit. At the start of your turn: Plant Elder. Show a grand symbolic still-life: one iconic object or pose that stands for the idea. Make it epic: glowing, richer detail, a grander composition.
3. "The Spirit Council" (skill): Plant Hawk. Plant Bear. Plant Elder. Apply 2 Spirit. Consume. Show a defensive or tactical moment, calm and clear. Make it epic: glowing, richer detail, a grander composition.
4. "Abyssal Totem" (skill): Plant Elder 2 times. Show a defensive or tactical moment, calm and clear. A little more dramatic and detailed.
5. "Ancestral Learning" (power): At the start of your turn: Draw 1 card. Show a grand symbolic still-life: one iconic object or pose that stands for the idea. A little more dramatic and detailed.
6. "Ancient Bastion" (skill): Gain 10 Barrier. Plant Bear 2 times. Show a defensive or tactical moment, calm and clear. A little more dramatic and detailed.
7. "Biased Grain" (power): Apply 3 Spirit. At the start of your turn: Lose 1 HP. Show a grand symbolic still-life: one iconic object or pose that stands for the idea. A little more dramatic and detailed.
8. "Blazing Eye" (attack): Deal 18 damage to ALL enemies. Awaken your first 3 totems. Show a dynamic strike caught mid-action. A little more dramatic and detailed.
9. "Carver's Engine" (power): At the start of your turn: Add a random card from your pool to your hand. Show a grand symbolic still-life: one iconic object or pose that stands for the idea. A little more dramatic and detailed.

NEGATIVE: text, letters, numbers, captions, labels, UI, card frames, borders, watermark, logo, photorealism, gritty texture, nudity, characters facing away.
```
