## Sheet `trapper-01` (legendary first)

```
Create ONE ILLUSTRATION SHEET of 9 separate card-art pictures for a card-battler game (like Slay the Spire), 2400 x 1800, no text.

Each picture has ONE clear subject in the middle that reads at thumbnail size, a simple scene behind it, light from the upper left.

WORLD: Vesper, a masked trapper with teal glow. A masked trapper. No companions: Traps, Exposed and patience. World: a moonlit ballroom of shadows: violet and teal mist, masks, mirrors, candelabras.

THE PICTURES, in this exact order, left to right, top row first. Use the card name and its effect only to decide what to draw; never write the name:
1. "Masquerade" (power): Whenever one of your Traps springs: Draw 2 cards. Gain 1 Mana. Deal 4 damage to ALL enemies. Show a grand symbolic still-life: one iconic object or pose that stands for the idea. Make it epic: glowing, richer detail, a grander composition.
2. "The Long Ambush" (snare): At the start of the enemy turn: Deal 25 damage to ALL enemies. Apply 1 Stunned to ALL enemies. Show a hidden trap seen as it is being set, ominous. Make it epic: glowing, richer detail, a grander composition.

4. "Bait and Switch" (snare): When you or an ally is attacked: Cancel that attack. Deal 12 damage to the attacker. Show a hidden trap seen as it is being set, ominous. A little more dramatic and detailed.
5. "Between Worlds" (skill): Apply 2 Ghostly. Consume. Show a defensive or tactical moment, calm and clear. A little more dramatic and detailed.
6. "Blindside" (attack): Deal 22 damage. Apply 3 Exposed. Consume. Show a dynamic strike caught mid-action. A little more dramatic and detailed.
7. "Coup de Grace" (attack): Deal 16 (+6 per Exposed on the target) damage. Show a dynamic strike caught mid-action. A little more dramatic and detailed.
8. "Culling" (attack): Deal 6 (+3 per Exposed on each) damage to ALL enemies. Show a dynamic strike caught mid-action. A little more dramatic and detailed.
9. "Curtain Call" (skill): Draw 1 card. Return 2 random snare cards from your discard pile to your hand. Consume. Show a defensive or tactical moment, calm and clear. A little more dramatic and detailed.

NEGATIVE: text, letters, numbers, captions, labels, UI, card frames, borders, watermark, logo, photorealism, gritty texture, nudity, characters facing away.
```
